Brand Insights

Beyond the logo

Louis Vuitton

Rooted in trunk-making, LV pieces favor structure and durability. A safe first investment for buyers who want a bag they'll use for a decade.

Gucci

Under a string of bold creative directors, Gucci leans maximalist. It's the pick for buyers who want their bag to say something.

Chanel

Restrained, quilted, and consistently the strongest performer on resale. The choice for buyers thinking long-term.
