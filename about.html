Our Story

Built by people who love the craft.

Savgo began as a small collection of authenticated finds. It has grown into a trusted home for three of fashion's most enduring houses.

How We Started

A boutique built on trust, not trend.

Savgo opened its doors with a simple frustration: buying luxury leather goods online too often meant guessing. We set out to build the opposite — a boutique where every Louis Vuitton, Gucci, and Chanel piece is inspected, documented, and sold with full transparency.

What started as a single authenticator working from a small studio has become a team of specialists, stylists, and craftspeople who share one standard: if we wouldn't carry it ourselves, we won't sell it to you.
