Frequently Asked

Quick answers

How do I know a piece is authentic?

Every item ships with a signed authentication report detailing the exact checks performed, from hardware stamping to stitch count.

What is your return policy?

You have 14 days from delivery to return an unused item in its original packaging for a full refund.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to most countries with full tracking and insurance included at checkout.

Can I sell my bag to Savgo?

We accept consignment and outright purchase for select pieces. Email photos and details to sell@savgo.com to start.
