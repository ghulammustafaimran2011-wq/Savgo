Brand Introduction

Florentine craft, worn boldly

Founded in Florence in 1921, Gucci built its name on equestrian-inspired hardware — the horsebit, the green-red-green web stripe — motifs that still anchor the house today. Under successive creative directors, the label has stayed unmistakably itself while constantly reinventing its silhouette.

Every Gucci piece at Savgo is checked against serial tag stitching, hardware weight and lining pattern before listing.
