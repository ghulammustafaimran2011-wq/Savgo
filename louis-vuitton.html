Brand Introduction

Founded on the art of travel

Louis Vuitton began as a trunk-maker for Parisian nobility, and that engineering discipline still shapes every bag today — structured, durable, and built to be handed down. The Monogram canvas, introduced in 1896, remains one of fashion's most recognized signatures.

Every LV piece at Savgo is authenticated against date codes, hardware stamping and stitch pattern before it's listed.
